WHERE THE NUMBERS ON THE CUBESENSE SITE COME FROM
==============================================================================

The CubeSense site shows four headline figures on its home page, plus a set of
area cards underneath. None of those are typed in by hand — they are all counted
from the project data, which was carried over from the old Cube Holdings site.
This file checks that the counting still lines up with what the old site says,
and shows the working for each figure so you can re-do it yourself.

Checked on:  Mon Sep 07 2026 13:24:20 GMT+0600
The site:    https://cubesense.intelsensetech.com
Checked against: https://www.cubeholdingsltd.com

A note on how this file was made: it is written by scripts/verify-stats.mjs,
which fetches the old site fresh every time it runs and recomputes everything.
So none of the numbers below were copied by hand, and re-running the script is
how you check whether anything has drifted since today.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE SITE SHOWS                   OURS    THEIRS  
38+  Projects delivered & underway    38      38      MATCH
357+ Apartments planned               357     357     MATCH
4    Prime Dhaka addresses            4       4       MATCH
14+  Years building                   14      14      MATCH

Everything agrees. The detail behind each line is below, one section per figure.


1. "38+ PROJECTS DELIVERED & UNDERWAY"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Where it comes from:
  https://www.cubeholdingsltd.com/sitemap.xml

How it is counted:
  The old site gives every project its own top-level page, so the sitemap is the
  cleanest list of them. I took every top-level URL and threw out the ones that
  are section or landing pages rather than projects — that is:
    blog, featured, upcoming, ongoing, handed-over, contact-us, career,
    project-search, all-brochures, want-to-know-about-projects,
    client-stories-projects, landowner-partnerships, about-us,
    terms-and-conditions.
  What is left is one URL per project.

What came back:
  The old site publishes 38 project pages. Our data has 38.
  I did not stop at the totals matching, because two different sets of 38 would
  still add up — so I compared them slug by slug:
    on their site but missing from ours: nothing
    in ours but not on their site:       nothing
  MATCH — the same 38 projects on both sides.

Worth knowing:
  The real number is exactly 38. The "+" is just how the stat is styled;
  it is not padding a rounded figure.


2. "357+ APARTMENTS PLANNED"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Where it comes from:
  https://www.cubeholdingsltd.com/<project>
  — all 38 project pages; every one is listed at the end of this file

How it is counted:
  Each project page has an "Apartments" field. I read that number off all 38
  pages and added them up.

What came back:
  Their pages add up to 357. Ours add up to 357.
  Projects where the two disagree: 0.
  MATCH.

Worth knowing — please read this one:
  This total is not published anywhere on the old site. Nobody at Cube Holdings
  ever wrote down 357; it is a sum we are computing from their per-project
  figures. Every part of it traces back to their pages, but the total itself is
  ours. It also counts apartments in upcoming and ongoing buildings, which is
  why the label says "planned" rather than "built" or "sold". If you would
  rather the site not present a derived number this prominently, that is a
  reasonable call and easy to change.


3. "4 PRIME DHAKA ADDRESSES"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Where it comes from:
  https://www.cubeholdingsltd.com/about-us  — the paragraph naming the neighbourhoods
  plus the address line on each of the 38 project pages

How it is counted:
  Group all the projects by area and count how many distinct areas there are.
  I then went back to each project page separately and confirmed its address
  really does mention the area we filed it under, so the grouping is not just
  our own labelling repeated back to itself.

What came back:
  Bashundhara R/A (32), Uttara (4), Banani (1), Jolshiri Abashon (1)
  Projects whose page did not back up their area: 0.
  MATCH — and the about-us paragraph names exactly these four, no more.


4. "14+ YEARS BUILDING"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Where it comes from:
  https://www.cubeholdingsltd.com/               says 2012
  https://www.cubeholdingsltd.com/about-us       says 2012

How it is counted:
  2026 minus 2012 is 14.
  The two pages word it differently — the home page says "Established 2012" and
  about-us says "Since 2012" — so I checked both rather than trusting one.
  They agree, which is why this reads MATCH.

Worth knowing:
  This one moves on its own. It is worked out from the founding year in
  src/lib/site.ts, not written into the page, so it will say 15 next January
  without anyone touching it. That is deliberate, but it does mean the number
  in this file has a shelf life.


5. THE AREA CARDS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Where it comes from:
  the area on each of the 38 project pages — same source as section 3

How it is counted:
  Group the projects by area and count each group. As a sanity check, the cards
  have to add back up to the project count in section 1, otherwise a project has
  gone missing or been counted twice.

What came back:
  Banani                 1
  Bashundhara R/A        32
  Jolshiri Abashon       1
  Uttara                 4
  adds up to             38  — same as the 38 projects, as it should be

Worth knowing:
  The spread is very lopsided — 32 of 38 sit in Bashundhara R/A, and two of the
  four areas have a single project each. The cards are accurate, but showing
  four areas side by side does imply a more even footprint than there is.


6. ONGOING / UPCOMING / HANDED OVER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Where it comes from:
  https://www.cubeholdingsltd.com/ongoing      (and /ongoing/residential, /ongoing/commercial)
  https://www.cubeholdingsltd.com/upcoming     (and /upcoming/residential, /upcoming/commercial)
  https://www.cubeholdingsltd.com/handed-over  (and /handed-over/residential, /handed-over/commercial)

  Rather than trust a status field, I fetched each listing page and recorded

SO, WHERE DOES THAT LEAVE US
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Everything checks out. All 38 projects match slug for slug, all 38 apartment
counts match one by one, the status split matches, and both of their pages
agree the company started in 2012.
In short: 38 projects, 357 apartments, 4 areas, trading since 2012.

Two things I would still flag, neither of them an error:

  The 357 is a total we compute, not one they publish. See section 2. It is
  correct arithmetic on their numbers, but it is our claim, not theirs.

  "Projects delivered & underway" counts all 38 project pages, upcoming ones
  included. If you want that headline to mean genuinely finished and handed
  over, the honest number is 10. Which figure to show is a business
  decision rather than a data one, so I have left it as is.

To check any of this yourself, open the links above, or re-run:
  node scripts/verify-stats.mjs
It refetches their site and rewrites this file from scratch, so if their
numbers change, this file will say so.
